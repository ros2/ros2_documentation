Analysis
========

Sometimes you need tools to analyze the data from your robot to understand what it means. This article summarizes the ROS developer tools and guidance available to help with analysis.

**Area: analysis, tools | Content-type: about | Experience: beginner, intermediate, expert**

   :local:

Summary
-------   

Capturing the data from your robot is just the first step. Analysis tools in ROS help you go further by making sense of that data and revealing insights at the right level of detail.

Core ROS packages:

* `tracetools <https://docs.ros.org/en/kilted/p/tracetools/>`_: Trace points for creating and debugging execution traces.
