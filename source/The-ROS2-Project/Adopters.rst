ROS 2 adopters
==============

.. short-description::
   ROS is used by organizations and projects across industry, research, education, and the wider robotics community.
   This article provides a community-maintained directory of ROS adopters and explains how you can add your own organization or project.

.. showmeta::
   :order: area, contentType, experience
   :labels: area=Area, contentType=Content type, experience=Level

.. contents:: Table of Contents
   :depth: 2
   :local:

This page showcases organizations and projects using ROS in any capacity.
It is a community-maintained, self-reported directory.
If you use ROS, we encourage you to add your project.

To add your organization or project, see :doc:`Adopters/Add-Your-Project`.
