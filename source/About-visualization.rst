Visualization
=============

Sometimes you need to visualize what your robot is sensing and planning to better develop, test, and debug your system. This article summarizes the ROS developer tools and guidance available to help with visualization.


**Area: visualization, tools | Content-type: about | Experience: beginner, intermediate, expert**

.. contents:: Table of Contents
   :local:

Summary
-------  

It is much easier to develop, test, and debug a robotics system when you can understand what the robot is seeing, experiencing, and planning. Visualization tools transform the raw data from a robot into visual forms understandable by people.
