Middleware
==========

.. short-description::
   The middleware is the communication layer that ROS uses to discover nodes and exchange data between them, such as DDS or Zenoh.
   These articles explain how ROS integrates with middleware implementations, compare the available vendors, and show you how to create your own ``rmw`` implementation.
