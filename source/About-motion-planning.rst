Motion planning
===============

Motion planning capabilities enable you to control complex chains of actuators and links by focusing on where you want the end effector to be.
This article summarizes ROS developer tools and guidance available to help with motion planning.

**Area: motion-planning, capabilities | Content-type: about | Experience: beginner, intermediate, expert**

.. contents:: Table of Contents
   :local:

Summary
-------

Motion planning capabilities enable the control of complex chains of actuators and links, such as those found in industrial manipulators.
These capabilities let you think about where you want the end effector to be and not worry about controlling every actuator individually.
